# Inference Korea

[Website and study timetable](https://songys.github.io/inference-korea/)

Inference Korea is a study community focused on inference engineering: how to run
AI models efficiently and reliably.

## Study format

We plan to meet weekly to discuss *Inference Engineering* by Patrick Kiely and related
research papers, covering topics such as GPU architecture, inference engines,
quantization, model parallelism, and deployment.

Members will take turns presenting book chapters and papers.

We are also planning individual projects and hands-on sessions.

## Timetable

The website includes the 10-session timetable from October 24 to December 26,
2026, with chapter ranges, presenters, reference papers, and videos.
Meetings are scheduled for Saturdays at 8:00 PM KST (tentative).
