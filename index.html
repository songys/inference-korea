Inference Korea

Inference Korea is a study community focused on inference engineering: how to run AI models efficiently and reliably.

Study format

We plan to meet weekly to discuss Inference Engineering by Patrick Kiely and related research papers, covering topics such as GPU architecture, inference engines, quantization, model parallelism, and deployment.

Members will take turns presenting book chapters and papers.

We are also planning individual projects and hands-on sessions.

Study timetable

Every Saturday at 8:00 PM KST (tentative).

Primary textbook: Kiely, P. (2026). Inference Engineering. Baseten Books.
